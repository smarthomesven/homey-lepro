Thank you for installing the Lepro app! Some setup is required for this app to function properly.


The Lepro mobile app only allows one active session per account. This means that when Homey logs in to your Lepro account, your phone app will log out. Therefore, it is recommended to create a second Lepro account for Homey and share your lights with that account. This way, you can keep using both the Lepro mobile app and Homey at the same time.


If any light model that you use is missing, or your light isn't found, please let me know in the forum topic!
